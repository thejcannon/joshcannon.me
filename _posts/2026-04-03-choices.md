---
title: Choices (a poem)
subtitle: Nothing clever here. Just some rhyming honesty.
---

After I left Anthropic (partially reasoned below), I found I had creativity and hobbies again.
I sprouted some new ones, though, like writing (mostly "silly") poetry. 
Those who know me know I love wordplay, including rhymes, so this has been a fun activity for me personally
to get to enjoy taking English apart and putting it back together (the sillyness comes from doing to English
what a child might do to a Mr Potato Head doll).

The following is a poem, but not a silly one, which I'm using to help me justify (to myself and others) my choices:
past, present, and hopefully future. Enjoy!

> Some people stand on the shoulders of giants,</br>
> while others go run with the bulls.</br>
> I've seen if I tried I could stand and could run,</br>
> but it leaves my glass only half full.
>
> Well, that's not quite true, see, its filled to the brim.</br>
> Cuz' my glass truly doth runneth over.</br>
> But only half full with the blood of my being,</br>
> while the other's half empty with water.
>
> I think I'll gulp less from the Fountain of Knowledge,</br>
> the beverage which sates not the soul.</br>
> And hope that the liquid which flow froms my heart,</br>
> will full cups before I'm too old.
